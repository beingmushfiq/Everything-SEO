## 1. The Scientific SEO Lifecycle
True SEO gains are generated through controlled, documented experiments rather than superficial tricks. Every test must document:

```text
[HYPOTHESIS] ➔ [BASELINE METRICS] ➔ [SINGLE CONTROLLED CHANGE] ➔ [30-DAY OBSERVATION] ➔ [STATISTICAL OUTCOME]
```

---

## 2. Standardized Experiment Card Template

### Experiment Spec: EX-2026-001 (Title Tag CTR Optimization)
- **Target URL**: `/services/technical-seo`
- **Hypothesis**: Replacing a generic title (`Technical SEO Services`) with a benefit-driven title containing an agency value proposition will increase Organic CTR by >= 35% without hurting average position.
- **Baseline (30 Days Prior)**:
  - Impressions: 18,400
  - Clicks: 312
  - Average CTR: 1.70%
  - Average Position: 4.8
- **Implementation**:
  - *New Title*: `Technical SEO Consulting & Architecture Audits | DevCenterPoint`
  - *Date Deployed*: `2026-10-01`
- **Measurement Criteria**: Google Search Console Clicks, Impressions, CTR after 30 days.
- **Result Log**:
  - Impressions: 19,200 (+4.3%)
  - Clicks: 580 (+85.9%)
  - Average CTR: 3.02% (+1.32% absolute / +77% relative)
- **Decision**: Win confirmed. Roll out similar title format across all service category pages.
