# On-Page Title Tags, Headings & Meta Optimization Standard

> **Category**: On-page SEO  
> **Status**: Verified Standard  
> **Target Audience**: Content Engineers, SEO Copywriters

---

## 1. Title Tag Specifications
- **Pixel Width & Character Limits**: Keep desktop titles between 45 and 65 characters (approximately 580 pixels).
- **Front-Loading**: Place primary keyword intent within the first 35 characters.
- **Brand Consistency**: End titles with a standardized delimiter and brand name:  
  `Primary Keyword Phrase - Secondary Value Modifier | DevCenterPoint`
- **Zero Duplication**: Every canonical URL must maintain a mathematically unique title tag.

---

## 2. Heading Architecture (`H1` - `H6`)
- **Single `H1` Rule**: Each HTML document must possess exactly one `<h1>` tag matching the core thesis of the page.
- **Logical Nesting**: Do not skip heading levels (e.g., jumping from `<h2>` directly into `<h4>`).
- **Entity Inclusion**: Naturally embed secondary semantic entities and LSI keywords in `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags.

---

## 3. Meta Descriptions & CTR Optimization
- **Character Benchmark**: 120 - 155 characters.
- **Actionable Call-to-Action**: Always incorporate an active verb (`Discover`, `Explore`, `Learn`, `Audit`, `Compare`).
- **Rich Snippet Eligibility**: Use structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Article) alongside compelling metadata to increase SERP real estate.
