# Ahrefs Tool Operations

## A. Purpose
The Ahrefs Tool Operations skill establishes the rigorous standard operating procedures (SOPs) for leveraging the Ahrefs SEO intelligence platform. Ahrefs possesses one of the world's largest commercial web-crawling databases (AhrefsBot) and clickstream processing engines. While Ahrefs metrics (Domain Rating [DR], URL Rating [UR], and Keyword Difficulty [KD]) are proprietary third-party estimates rather than official search engine signals, the platform provides unmatched intelligence for competitive backlink auditing, keyword gap analysis, content exploration, and link intersection mining. This skill defines precise workflows to maximize research value while avoiding data misinterpretation.

## B. Trigger Conditions
Invoke this skill when:
1. Conducting competitive backlink profile audits or forensic backlink gap investigations.
2. Sizing market search volume, keyword difficulty, and Parent Topic clusters in Keywords Explorer.
3. Discovering historical top-performing content and link magnets across competitor domains via Site Explorer.
4. Performing Link Intersect analysis to identify shared referring domains linking to multiple competitors.
5. Auditing multi-domain competitive metrics in bulk via the Batch Analysis tool.

Upstream dependencies: [Keyword Discovery](../../Keyword%20Research/Keyword%20Discovery/SKILL.md) and [Digital PR](../../Link%20Building/Digital%20PR/SKILL.md).

## F. Core Concepts and Decision Principles
1. **Understanding Ahrefs Metrics Correctly**:
   - **Domain Rating (DR)**: A logarithmic (0-100) measure of a domain's relative backlink profile strength based on the quantity and quality of external referring domains. **DR is NOT a Google ranking factor**. It is a relative benchmark against other web domains.
   - **URL Rating (UR)**: Measures the backlink strength of a specific single page on a 0-100 scale, taking internal link PageRank flow into account. UR correlates much more strongly with ranking ability than DR.
   - **Keyword Difficulty (KD)**: A calculated estimate (0-100) of how many referring domains a page needs to reach Google Top 10. KD does not evaluate content quality, search intent, or on-page relevance.
2. **The Traffic Potential (TP) Metric**:
   - Looking solely at a single keyword's monthly volume is misleading because high-ranking pages rank for hundreds of long-tail queries simultaneously.
   - Ahrefs **Traffic Potential (TP)** estimates the total monthly organic traffic generated by the #1 ranking page across *all* keywords it ranks for. Always prioritize by Traffic Potential over individual keyword volume.
3. **Verification of Data Staleness**:
   - Ahrefs crawls billions of pages daily, but niche or low-authority pages may only be crawled once every few months. Always check the **"Last Crawled" date** when evaluating specific backlinks.

## G. Operational Workflow

```
[Define Target Domain & Competitors] -> [Site Explorer: Backlinks & Top Pages Audit]
                 |                                                |
                 v                                                v
   [Keywords Explorer: Parent Topic & TP] -> [Link Intersect: Mine Shared Referring Domains]
                 |                                                |
                 v                                                v
    [Batch Analysis: Screen Outreach Targets] -> [Export Cleaned CSV Data for Action]
```

### Step 1: Site Explorer Competitor Backlink Forensics
1. Enter the competitor's root domain into **Site Explorer**.
2. Select **Mode: `*.domain.com/*`** (captures all subdomains).
3. Navigate to **Backlink Profile > Referring Domains**:
   - Filter by: `DoFollow only`, `DR >= 30`.
   - Sort by: `Domain Rating (Descending)`.
   - Identify the authoritative media outlets, industry blogs, and university resource pages pointing to your competitor.
4. Navigate to **Pages > Best by Links**:
   - Filter by: `HTTP 200 OK`.
   - Identify which of your competitor's content assets attract the highest number of backlinks (see [Linkable Assets](../../Link%20Building/Linkable%20Assets/SKILL.md)).

### Step 2: Keywords Explorer Intent & Cluster Mining
1. In **Keywords Explorer**, enter 5-10 core seed keywords.
2. Review the **Parent Topic**:
   - The Parent Topic indicates the highest-volume keyword that the top-ranking page for your query also ranks for.
   - *Example*: Searching `how to backup postgres database` may reveal a Parent Topic of `postgresql backup` with 10x higher search demand, showing you how to name your primary pillar page.
3. Check the **Clicks vs. Volume** metric:
   - Identify if the query suffers from a high Zero-Click rate. If a keyword has 10,000 searches but only 3,000 clicks, factor in the click discount.
4. Review **Matching Terms > Questions**:
   - Extract the top 20 questions users ask around the entity to build your H2/H3 outline (see [Headings Optimization](../../On-page%20SEO/Headings%20Optimization/SKILL.md)).

### Step 3: Link Intersect Mining (The High-Probability Target List)
1. In the top navigation, open **More > Link Intersect**.
2. Enter Competitor 1, Competitor 2, and Competitor 3 in the "Show me who is linking to these targets" fields.
3. Enter your own domain in the "But not linking to" field.
4. Click **Show link opportunities**:
   - This generates a list of websites that link to multiple competitors in your space, but have not yet linked to you.
   - If a website links to 3 of your competitors, they have an established editorial habit of covering your industry; pitching them with [Digital PR](../../Link%20Building/Digital%20PR/SKILL.md) or [Guest Posting](../../Link%20Building/Guest%20Posting/SKILL.md) yields an outreach conversion rate $> 3\times$ higher than cold prospects.

### Step 4: Batch Analysis for Bulk Outreach Vetting
When curating a list of 100 potential outreach websites:
1. Open **More > Batch Analysis**.
2. Paste the list of 100 domain URLs (one per line).
3. Export the results and filter by:
   - `Domain Rating (DR) >= 40`.
   - `Organic Traffic >= 2,000 / month`.
   - `Referring Domains (Live) >= 150`.
4. Discard any target domain with zero organic traffic, regardless of its reported DR.

### Step 5: Programmatic API Automation (Python)
If your organization holds an Ahrefs Enterprise plan, query the Ahrefs API v3:
```python
import requests

API_TOKEN = "YOUR_AHREFS_API_TOKEN"
headers = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {API_TOKEN}", "Accept": "application/json"}

# Query domain rating and backlink metrics
target_domain = "example.com"
url = f"https://api.ahrefs.com/v3/site-explorer/overview?target={target_domain}&mode=subdomains"

resp = requests.get(url, headers=headers).json()
overview = resp.get('overview', {})
print(f"Ahrefs Domain Rating: {overview.get('domain_rating')}")
print(f"Total Live Backlinks: {overview.get('backlinks')}")
print(f"Total Referring Domains: {overview.get('refdomains')}")
```

## I. Prioritization Framework
Score competitor backlink targets for outreach using the **Ahrefs Link Gravity Score (ALGS)**:

$$\text{ALGS} = \text{Domain Rating (DR)} \times \log_{10}(\text{Monthly Organic Traffic} + 1) \times \text{Intersect Count (1-5)}$$

Prioritize domains with high organic traffic that link to at least 2 competitors.

## J. Deliverables & Specifications
Deliver an **Ahrefs Competitive Intelligence Dossier**:
1. `domain_metrics`: DR, UR, total referring domains, organic keyword count.
2. `competitor_link_intersect`: List of top 25 websites linking to 2+ competitors but not the client.
3. `top_competitor_content_assets`: URLs with the highest referring domain counts.
4. `keyword_targets_matrix`: Keywords sorted by Traffic Potential (TP), KD, and Parent Topic.
5. `remediation_action_items`: Specific outreach targets and content gap opportunities.

## K. Quality Assurance Checklist
- [ ] Verified that search volume and KD correspond to the client's target country database (not global aggregate).
- [ ] Confirmed that prospective link targets have stable, growing organic traffic (not hit by penalties).
- [ ] Evaluated Traffic Potential rather than individual keyword volume.
- [ ] Filtered for DoFollow links when evaluating competitive backlink profiles.
- [ ] Acknowledged that Ahrefs data is third-party estimation and validated critical metrics against Search Console.

## L. Failure Modes and Anti-Patterns
- **Treating DR as a Google Ranking Factor**: Believing that increasing your Ahrefs DR score automatically increases Google rankings. Google does not use DR; Google uses its own PageRank algorithms.
- **The "0 KD" Blind Spot**: Assuming a KD 0 keyword is easy to rank for without checking the live SERP, missing the fact that 3 government (.gov) websites occupy the top spots.
- **Ignoring Disavow File Realities**: Using Ahrefs "toxic backlink" third-party metrics to mass-disavow thousands of links in Google. Google officially advises against using third-party disavow tools, as Googlebot ignores spam links automatically.

## M. AI-Agent Operating Instructions
1. An AI agent must parse Ahrefs CSV exports using Pandas, filtering out nofollow and zero-traffic domains automatically.
2. Use the `Parent Topic` field to group keyword lists into master thematic clusters before assigning content briefs.
3. Never cite Ahrefs metrics as definitive Google measurements; always explicitly label them as "Ahrefs Third-Party Estimates".
