# 150+ SEO Tactics & Scientific Experimentation Library

## A. Purpose
The 150+ SEO Tactics skill transforms the popular concept of "SEO Hacks" into a rigorous, evidence-tiered experimentation framework. Search engine optimization is an empirical science governed by complex mathematical algorithms, machine learning models, and user interaction feedback. Superficial "hacks" copied from social media frequently result in wasted resources, algorithmic devaluation, or manual spam actions. This skill provides an auditable, categorized repository of proven optimization tactics—each classified into strict validity tiers—paired with a formal scientific testing protocol (Hypothesis, Baseline, Cohort Testing, Confounder Control, Statistical Significance).

## B. Trigger Conditions
Invoke this skill when:
1. Brainstorming high-leverage growth experiments to break through organic traffic plateaus.
2. Formulating a quarterly organic optimization sprint with quantifiable ROI targets.
3. Vetting tactics proposed by team members or external consultants against evidence-based risk standards.
4. Structuring controlled A/B or split-testing experiments on large programmatic, eCommerce, or editorial sites.

Upstream dependencies: [CTR Optimization](../../On-page%20SEO/CTR%20Optimization/SKILL.md), [Internal Linking](../../On-page%20SEO/Internal%20Linking/SKILL.md), and [Content Refresh](../../Content%20Optimization/Content%20Refresh/SKILL.md).

## D. Required Inputs
1. **Target Experiment Cohort**: A group of similar URLs (e.g. 50 eCommerce product pages or 30 blog guides).
2. **Control Cohort**: A matched group of comparable URLs that remain unchanged to measure macro algorithm/seasonal shifts.
3. **GSC Performance Baseline**: Minimum 60 days of daily impression, click, and position data.
4. **Target Hypothesis**: The specific linguistic, structural, or technical change to be tested.

## F. Core Concepts and Decision Principles
1. **The 6-Tier Evidence Classification Taxonomy**:
   Every tactic considered or deployed must be categorized into one of six explicit tiers:
   - **Tier 1: Established Practice**: Documented by Google Search Central or formal RFC web standards (e.g., self-referencing canonicals, mobile-friendly design, 301 redirects for moved pages).
   - **Tier 2: Evidence-Supported Opportunity**: Corroborated by broad empirical industry testing and correlation studies (e.g., adding current year to title tags on software comparison queries, reducing LCP below 2.5s).
   - **Tier 3: Context-Dependent Technique**: Effective in specific niches, but ineffective or counterproductive in others (e.g., extreme programmatic SEO generation, FAQ accordion markup).
   - **Tier 4: Experimental Hypothesis**: Plausible theoretical optimization requiring controlled testing before scaling (e.g., bolding first sentence of every subsection, specific internal link density ratios).
   - **Tier 5: Historical Tactic (Deprecated)**: Tactics that worked in 2012-2018 but are now ignored by modern algorithms (e.g., Meta Keywords tag, exact-match keyword density formulas, AMP pages for mobile).
   - **Tier 6: Unsupported or Risky Claim**: Violates Google Spam Policies or is mathematically debunked (e.g., PBNs, automated article spinning, CTR manipulation bots). **NEVER DEPLOY**.
2. **Causal Impact & Confounding Factors**:
   - Never conclude that a change worked simply because traffic went up after the change. Organic search is affected by Google algorithm updates, seasonal holidays, competitor changes, and macro news cycles.
   - True scientific testing requires a **Control Group** or **Causal Inference Modeling** to prove statistical significance.

### Category 1: On-Page & Snippet Optimization
1. **Tactic 01 (Tier 1 - Established)**: Front-load the exact primary keyword in the first 3 words of the `<title>` tag.
2. **Tactic 02 (Tier 2 - Evidence-Supported)**: Inject bracketed modifiers (`[Case Study]`, `(Step-by-Step)`, `[Data Table]`) at the end of title tags to boost CTR by 15-25%.
3. **Tactic 03 (Tier 2 - Evidence-Supported)**: Update the calendar year (`2026`) in titles and meta descriptions for time-sensitive commercial software queries every January.
4. **Tactic 04 (Tier 1 - Established)**: Ensure exactly one semantic `<h1>` tag exists per page and semantically mirrors the `<title>` tag to prevent automated Google rewrites.
5. **Tactic 05 (Tier 2 - Evidence-Supported)**: Provide a 45-word direct declarative definition paragraph immediately beneath question H2 headings to target Featured Snippets.
6. **Tactic 06 (Tier 1 - Established)**: Use HTML `<table>` elements with explicit `<th>` column headers for comparison data rather than plain paragraphs or images.
7. **Tactic 07 (Tier 2 - Evidence-Supported)**: Bold the lead 2-3 words of bulleted list items to increase visual scannability and mobile dwell time.
8. **Tactic 08 (Tier 1 - Established)**: Add descriptive alt text that explains visual chart data to improve image accessibility and Google Images indexing.
9. **Tactic 09 (Tier 2 - Evidence-Supported)**: Eliminate un-escaped double quotes (`"`) from meta description tags to prevent premature snippet truncation.
10. **Tactic 10 (Tier 3 - Context-Dependent)**: Include a clickable Table of Contents with jump-link anchors (`#section`) to trigger sitelinks in search results.

18. **Tactic 18 (Tier 2 - Evidence-Supported)**: Build hub-and-spoke topical clusters where subtopics cross-link to sibling pages within the same topical silo.
19. **Tactic 19 (Tier 3 - Context-Dependent)**: Add a "Related Reading" card at the end of articles containing 3 contextually relevant cluster posts.

## H. The Scientific SEO Experimentation Protocol

```
[Define Test Hypothesis] -> [Select Matched Treatment vs. Control Cohorts]
           |                                         |
           v                                         v
 [Establish 60-Day Baseline] -> [Deploy Isolated Change to Treatment Group Only]
           |                                         |
           v                                         v
 [Run 28-Day Causal Impact Test] -> [Determine Stat-Sig (p < 0.05) & Roll Out / Revert]
```

### Experiment Card Specification
For every tactic being tested, fill out the standard **Experiment Card**:
1. **Hypothesis**: "If we update title tags on our 30 PostgreSQL guide pages to include bracketed modifier `[2026 Guide]`, then organic CTR will increase by $\ge 15\%$ because it visually signals technical freshness on live SERPs."
2. **Treatment Cohort**: 30 specific guide URLs.
3. **Control Cohort**: 30 matched guide URLs covering MySQL/Redis that remain completely unchanged.
4. **Baseline Period**: 60 days prior to deployment.
5. **Measurement Period**: 28 days post-indexation.
6. **Confounding Variables Logged**: Core algorithm update deployed on Day 12; tracked across both treatment and control.
7. **Observed Results**: Treatment group CTR increased from 3.2% to 4.1% (+28%). Control group CTR moved from 3.1% to 3.2% (+3%).
8. **Statistical Significance**: $p = 0.021$ ($p < 0.05$, statistically significant win).
9. **Decision**: Roll out bracketed year modifiers sitewide across all remaining technical documentation.

## I. Prioritization Framework
Score experimental candidates using the **P.I.E. Framework**:

$$\text{P.I.E. Score} = \frac{\text{Potential Impact (1-10)} + \text{Implementation Ease (1-10)} + \text{Confidence (1-10)}}{3}$$

- *Potential*: What is the traffic/revenue upside if this works?
- *Ease*: How many engineering/copywriting hours does it take to test?
- *Confidence*: What is the evidence tier (Tier 1/2 = 9-10; Tier 4 = 4-5)?

## J. Deliverables & Specifications
Deliver a **Scientific SEO Experimentation Plan**:
1. `experiment_name`: Unique test identifier.
2. `tactic_id_and_tier`: Mapped to catalog tactic and evidence tier.
3. `treatment_urls`: List of URLs receiving the change.
4. `control_urls`: Matched baseline URLs remaining unchanged.
5. `metric_tracked`: CTR, Impressions, Clicks, or Average Position.
6. `decision_criteria`: Quantified threshold for declaring a win or revert.

## K. Quality Assurance Checklist
- [ ] No Tier 5 (Deprecated) or Tier 6 (Toxic/Risky) tactics approved.
- [ ] Control group established to isolate external algorithm updates and seasonal swings.
- [ ] Baseline data logged for at least 30-60 days before deploying changes.
- [ ] Test runs for at least 28 days to capture day-of-week traffic cycles.
- [ ] Rollback plan documented to revert changes within 24 hours if metrics degrade.

## L. Failure Modes and Anti-Patterns
- **The "Everything Everywhere All At Once" Mistake**: Changing the title tags, rewriting the body text, adding schema, and redesigning the layout all on the same day. If traffic changes, you have zero idea which variable caused the movement.
- **Testing Without a Control Group**: Claiming a title tag test was a massive win because clicks went up in December, failing to notice that seasonal holiday demand caused the entire industry's traffic to double.
- **Deploying Tier 6 Hacks**: Using automated PBN links or hidden text tricks. Short-term algorithmic gains are inevitably followed by permanent manual penalties and domain de-indexation.

## M. AI-Agent Operating Instructions
1. An AI agent must strictly enforce the 6-tier classification taxonomy on any proposed optimization tactic.
2. Ensure treatment and control cohorts are matched by baseline impression volume and intent category before initializing an experiment.
3. Calculate statistical significance ($p$-values) mathematically rather than eyeballing trend lines.

## N. Success Metrics & Verification Windows
- **Experiment Win Rate**: $\ge 60\%$ of tested Tier 2 and Tier 4 hypotheses generate positive, statistically significant organic lift ($p < 0.05$).
- **Risk Containment**: 0 manual penalties or catastrophic traffic drops introduced through unvetted tactics.
