5. **Section E: Input Requirements & Prerequisites** — Exact datasets, API credentials, tools, and baselines needed.

7. **Section G: Finding Interpretation & Diagnostic Heuristics** — Threshold matrices, diagnostic decision trees.
8. **Section H: Prioritization & Impact Matrix** — ICE / RICE scoring tailored to the specific capability.
9. **Section I: Deliverable Specification** — Format, markdown templates, schemas, and schemas of the work product.
10. **Section J: Quality Assurance & Verification Checklist** — Mandatory pre-flight validation checklist.
11. **Section K: Common Failure Modes & Remediation** — Diagnostic tables of common traps and concrete fixes.
12. **Section L: Interconnected Skill Graph** — Markdown links with relative URIs to prerequisite and downstream skills.
13. **Section M: Agentic Automation & LLM Orchestration** — Prompts, temperature settings, and validation guardrails.
